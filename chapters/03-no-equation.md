# III. No Equation of State

## Thesis

OODA-loop arithmetic and Lanchester++ score capabilities, not control.
No closed form tells you when the human leaves the chain - same negative
result as the sister book's Chapter III (local metrics neither confirm nor
rule out the transition). So we track observables, not curves.

## Beats (expand)

- Why decision-cycle math mislocates us (faster loops can still have
  human gates; slow loops can be fully delegated).
- The measurable substitute: approval latency vs strike latency, dated.
- Kill-box doctrine as the historical control instrument - and its decay.
