# X. The Book in the Corpus

## The coupling term (sister book: on-ai-takeover, 2026-09-09)

The bad endings in this book and in `on-ai-takeover` are not independent
risks to be summed - they are the same trajectory viewed from the dugout
and from the lab. Hyperwar accelerates takeover's clock: weekly tit-for-tat
selection pressure spends generation-time floors fastest exactly where
safety cases are thinnest, because whatever keeps a human gate loses to
whatever doesn't, visibly, every Friday. Takeover lowers the cost of
hyperwar's worst rungs in return: once the delegating institution is itself
past rung 13, kill envelopes get generated at machine speed, not merely
executed at it. Consequences arrive on the faster of the two clocks. The
Coxon decade holds, possibly with margin the wrong way.
