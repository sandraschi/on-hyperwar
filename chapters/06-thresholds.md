# VI. Thresholds A-E as Attachment Points

## Thesis

Thresholds name who can still attach brakes and how - export chokepoints
(compute, optics), approval-gate doctrine, hotlines that function at
machine speed (and admission where none can), treaty language with
verification hooks.

## Thresholds (draft)

- A: Design authority (who may field autonomy - national policy).
- B: Audit floor (what must be logged/reviewable per release).
- C: Operational consent (command responsibility that still means something).
- D: Political consent (the governed agree to machine-speed war).
- E: Point of no return (rebuild of human control slower than the war).
