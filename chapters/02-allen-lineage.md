# II. Allen 2016-2024: What Survived Contact

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-09 (non-normative prose; Allen specifics need

## The prediction, before the models and before the war

In 2016 John Allen described hyperwar before modern AI existed: decision
speed plus algorithmic teaming as the coming revolution in warfare - not
better weapons but a faster loop, with human-machine teaming compressing
the distance between sensing and striking. The 2019 book systematized it;
the 2024 edition added chapters. Grade each claim now, because Ukraine
2022-2026 ran the experiment Allen could only theorize.

## Confirmed

**Weekly adaptation cadence.** Allen predicted accelerating
measure-countermeasure cycles; the record shows tit-for-tat invention on
roughly weekly rhythm - FPV, then jamming, then fiber, then the answer
to fiber - with Putin's adaptations arriving on the same cadence from
the other side. Nobody writes requirements documents at this speed.
The clock speed is the weapon, exactly as advertised.

**The commercial stack.** Allen's teaming vision assumed military
programs would build it. Instead Shenzhen built it: DJI-class airframes,
cameras, Orin-class compute modules, precision dual-use gimbals -
thousands per month, anyone's catalog. The substrate of hyperwar turned
out to be civilian, which nullifies every control regime written for
arsenals.

**The cost inversion.** Ukrainian DIY cruise missiles reaching Moscow at
two orders of magnitude below Northrop Grumman unit cost. When the
weapon is cheap, release authority gets cheap, and cheap decisions do
not wait for colonels. Allen priced speed; the war priced the missile.

## Unpredicted

**Fiber.** Kilometers of fiber spool defeating jamming by removing the
radio entirely - autonomy's hardest problem (contested comms) solved by
refusing it. Terminal recognition supervised, not piloted.

**VLA snipers.** Vision-language-action models on dual-use gimbals with
scopes: the recruit's red button migrating toward the sensor itself.

**Strategic reach from a tactical base.** DIY systems striking Moscow -
theater effects from workshop industry, collapsing the tactical-strategic
firewall Allen's framework inherited.
