# VIII. Terminal States

## Thesis

Saturation is not safety: a logistic plateau of capability with control
already delegated is still takeover of the kill chain. Enumerate terminal
configurations (stable deterrence, norms-locked autonomy, accidental
campaign, deliberate machine campaign, collapse of distinction) and the
rung at which each becomes reachable.
