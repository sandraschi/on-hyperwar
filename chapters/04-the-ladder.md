## Anatomy: why the hunter is a dog, not a man

The humanoid is a parade-ground answer to a mud question. Everything
below is public engineering, not instruction: the tradeoff table that
explains why Band V hardware converges on quadrupeds.

1. **Stability.** Four legs are statically stable; two are dynamically
   stable, i.e. a control loop that must never stop. Power loss or
   comms loss drops a biped and merely crouches a quadruped. Failure
   posture matters more than performance posture in a weapon.
2. **Energy.** Balance is a tax paid every second a biped stands.
   Quadruped locomotion skips it; same battery, longer mission.
3. **Payload geometry.** A low-slung chassis carries sensors and a
   gimbal near the ground with a low center of gravity. A humanoid
   spends mass on arms and hands whose dexterity the mission never uses.
4. **Mud.** The Ukraine substrate is mud half the year. Wheels and
   tracks bog down and whine; bipeds fall over; quadrupeds pick through
   rubble and stairs. The terrain votes, and it votes dog.
5. **Signature.** A quadruped goes prone. A humanoid is a 1.8-meter
   target that cannot stop being one. Low, still, cold: the entire
   survivability doctrine in three words, available only to the form
   factor that can lie down.
6. **Survivability.** Lose one leg of four and a tripod gait still
   moves. Lose one leg of two and the mission is over. Redundancy beats
   elegance under fire.
7. **Manufacture.** Fewer precision joints than a hand, and the
   commercial catalog already sells the chassis (Spot-class,
   Unitree-class, manufacturer specs public). No arsenal required -
   which is exactly why export regimes written for arsenals miss it
   (Chapter VI).
8. **Cost.** Order-of-magnitude below humanoid programs, same as every
   other inversion in this war (Chapter II). Cheap hunters deploy by
   the thousand; exquisite hunters deploy by the dozen. Numbers win
   the boxes that matter.

None of this teaches building. It explains buying - which is the
governance-relevant fact, because what can be bought cannot be
un-invented, only gated at chokepoints (optics, compute - Chapter VI).

## Anatomy: the swarm fights like a platoon, only faster

Platoon tactics are two centuries of compressed trial and error about
how small groups kill without dying: fix and flank, bounding overwatch,
encirclement, clearance. The swarm does not invent replacements. It
runs the same playbook at millisecond latency with perfect shared
memory - which changes everything while inventing nothing. Doctrinal
shape only; no procedures below, per Rule 2.

- **Fix and flank.** One element holds attention while others work the
  sides. Infantry does this with shouting, smoke, and timing drift. A
  swarm does it with synchronized clocks: the fixing element's entire
  job is to be seen, the flanking elements' entire job is to not be,
  coordinated tighter than any human section ever held.
- **Encirclement.** Fire teams converge so every exit is someone's
  sector. Humans approximate this with maps and radios; machines share
  one track picture, so the ring has no seams to slip.
- **Clearance roles.** Trench and dugout clearance - the infantry's
  most casualty-dense task - ports to machines first, because it is
  where human cost is highest and thegeometry is most constrained.
  Stated at role level deliberately; the procedures are not this book's
  business.
- **Sensor fusion with botmates.** Shared track files, triangulation
  from multiple apertures, handoff of contacts between units without a
  central plot. Radar and signals theory, decades public: the network
  sees what no single node sees.
- **Nonhierarchical mesh.** No platoon-leader node, no head of the
  snake. Task allocation by consensus among peers means decapitation
  strikes have nothing to decapitate - the oldest air-defense lesson
  (kill the C2) meets a C2 that is everywhere and nowhere. This is a
  property of the topology, not a secret.
- **Psyops against the remainder.** Loudspeaker surrender appeals are
  older than radio (WWII units, Gulf War leaflets). Machines do them
  persistently, in the target's language, at 0300, without fear,
  without sleep, without going home. The content is ancient; the
  stamina is new.
- **Stillness.** A hunter that powers down to a sensor watch in cover
  waits weeks on duty cycling plus solar trickle - months conditional
  on climate and canopy `[construction: field evidence needed; power
  budgets are claims until dated]`. Stillness inverts the economics of
  the hunt: the ambush no longer gets hungry, bored, or cold.

Each of these is a rung-sharpener for Band V: when a dated instance
shows one in the field, the corresponding rung stops being
`[construction]`. Chapter V holds the index.
