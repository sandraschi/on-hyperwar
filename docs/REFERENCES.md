## Epistemic Grading Taxonomy

Sources cited across `on-hyperwar` are audited to maintain strict demarcation between operational hardware reality and speculative defense concepts:

* **Tier 1 (Field Telemetry, Official Doctrine & Hardware Specifications):** Official military directives, manufacturer hardware datasheets (silicon, airframes, actuators), UN GGE session transcripts, and verified primary combat footage with corroborating geolocation.
* **Tier 2 (Strategic Studies & Operational Field Reports):** Authoritative defense institute assessments (RUSI, IISS, CSIS, CNA), peer-reviewed military journals (Naval Institute Proceedings, Parameters), and investigative field reporting.
* **Tier 3 (Doctrinal Forefront & `[construction]`):** Theoretical kill-chain models, speculative autonomous campaign architectures, and emerging tactical rungs awaiting multi-source confirmation.

## 1. Hyperwar Lineage & Foundational Doctrine (Tier 1 / Tier 2)

### [ALL17] Allen, John R., & Husain, Amir (2017)
* **Citation:** Allen, J. R., & Husain, A. (2017). "On Hyperwar." *Proceedings of the U.S. Naval Institute*, 143(7), 30–37.
* **Significance:** The seminal publication introducing the term "Hyperwar." Identifies biological human cognitive latency as the ultimate operational bottleneck; formulates the transition from human command to algorithmic human-machine teaming.

### [ALL19] Allen, John R., & Husain, Amir (2019)
* **Citation:** Allen, J. R., & Husain, A. (2019). *Hyperwar: Conflict and Competition in the AI Century*. Austin, TX: SparkCognition Press.
* **Core Thesis:** Exhaustive treatment of AI-driven combat, multi-domain command acceleration, and the geopolitical implications of autonomous weapons dominance.

### [ALL24] Allen, John R., & Husain, Amir (2024)
* **Citation:** Allen, J. R., & Husain, A. (2024). *Hyperwar: Conflict and Competition in the AI Century* (2nd Revised and Expanded Edition). Austin, TX: Cognitive Press.
* **Updates:** Incorporates foundation models, multimodal edge vision, generative mission planning, and initial observations from the Russo-Ukrainian war.

### [DOD23] United States Department of Defense (2023)
* **Citation:** U.S. Department of Defense. (2023). *Directive 3000.09: Autonomy in Weapon Systems*. Washington, DC: Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Policy. (Original 2012, revised January 2023).
* **Significance:** The formal baseline for Western "human-in-the-loop" and "appropriate levels of human judgment" mandates. Primary benchmark evaluated and critiqued in Chapter VII.

## 2. Foundations of Military Cybernetics & Attrition Physics (Historical)

### [BOY86] Boyd, John R. (1986)
* **Citation:** Boyd, J. R. (1986). *Patterns of Conflict*. Unpublished briefing slides, U.S. Marine Corps Command and Staff College. (198 pp.)
* **Core Contribution:** The Observe-Orient-Decide-Act (OODA) loop formulation; operational speed and friction as the decisive mechanisms of psychological and physical collapse.

### [LAN16] Lanchester, Frederick W. (1916)
* **Citation:** Lanchester, F. W. (1916). *Aircraft in Warfare: The Dawn of the Fourth Arm*. London: Constable and Company.
* **Core Contribution:** Formulation of the Linear and Square Laws of combat attrition. Analyzed in Chapter II regarding the radical cost inversion of modern FPV swarms vs. exquisite platforms.

### [KAH65] Kahn, Herman (1965)
* **Citation:** Kahn, H. (1965). *On Escalation: Metaphors and Scenarios*. New York: Frederick A. Praeger.
* **Methodological Precursor:** Architectural model for the 44-rung kill-chain escalation ladder.

## 3. The Ukraine Empirical Crucible & Hardware Substrates (Tier 1 / Tier 2)

### [NV-ORIN] NVIDIA Corporation (2023)
* **Citation:** NVIDIA Corporation. (2023). *NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano & Orin NX System-on-Module Technical Datasheet*. Santa Clara, CA: NVIDIA Enterprise Robotics Group.
* **Specifications:** Ampere architecture GPU with Tensor Cores, delivering 40 to 100 TOPS of INT8 compute at 7W–25W; primary silicon powering edge-AI optical terminal tracking on frontline drones.

### [UNI-B2] Unitree Robotics (2024)
* **Citation:** Hangzhou Yushu Technology Co., Ltd. (Unitree Robotics). (2024). *Unitree B2 Industrial Quadruped Robot Technical Datasheet and Payload Integration Manual*. Hangzhou, China.
* **Specifications:** Statically stable 4-legged quadruped, 50kg payload capacity, 5-hour endurance, high mud/water ingress resistance; commercial chassis adapted for stabilized gimbal weapon mounts.

### [RUSI23] Watling, Jack, & Reynolds, Nick (2023)
* **Citation:** Watling, J., & Reynolds, N. (2023). *Meatgrinder: Russian Tactics in the Second Year of the Ukraine War*. London: Royal United Services Institute for Defence and Security Studies (RUSI).
* **Empirical Focus:** Detailed field metrics on Ukrainian drone attrition (~10,000 airframes lost per month to EW), electronic spectrum saturation, and the failure of traditional vehicle assaults.

### [TURK26] Türk, Volker / UN OHCHR (2026)
* **Citation:** United Nations Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights. (2026). *Statement on the Field Employment of Fully Autonomous Lethal Weapon Systems in Eastern Europe*. Geneva: UN Press Briefing, 2026-09-08.
* **Context:** Official condemnation and alarm regarding confirmed deployments of comm-severed, machine-vision terminal strike drones operating without active human radio links.

### [CCW26] Group of Governmental Experts on LAWS (2026)
* **Citation:** United Nations Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW). (2026). *Draft Outcome Document of the 2026 Session of the Group of Governmental Experts on Emerging Technologies in the Area of Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems*. Geneva: UN Document CCW/GGE.1/2026/WP.4, 2026-09-05.
* **Context:** Non-binding declaration supported by 128 states; actively boycotted or weakened by the United States, the Russian Federation, China, and Ukraine. Primary exhibit for Threshold D failure in Chapter VI.

### [STEYR-ACR] Steyr-Mannlicher ACR (1987-1990) - Tier 1 hardware precursor
* **Citation:** Steyr-Mannlicher (1987-1990). *Steyr Advanced Combat Rifle (ACR), US Army Advanced Combat Rifle program entrant.* Designer Ulrich Zedrosser. See Wikipedia summary with US patents 4,739,570 et al.
* **Institutional fact:** Austrian bullpup flechette-rifle prototype for US ACR trials 1989/90. 3.2 kg unloaded, 780 mm overall. Program ended with no entrant beating M16A2 by required 100%. Follow-on: Steyr IWS 2000 long-range prototype in same family.
* **Constraint:** Hardware lineage only. No load data, no sabot fabrication detail, no primer detail. See Side - Glossary in this book for `flechette`, `sabot`, `bullpup` definitions.

### [ANTH26] Anthropic Frontier Red Team / Threat Intelligence (2026-09) - Tier 2
* **Citation:** Anthropic (2026-09). *Intelligence Targeting + Conventional Weapons Capabilities*. Frontier Red Team evals + Threat Intelligence Report September 2026 companion. https://www.anthropic.com/research/intelligence-targeting-conventional-weapons-capabilities
* **Significance:** Lab signal for control-centric thesis: reports AI substitution for scarce analyst/engineer labor at find/fix and terminal steps; reports observed misuse in surveillance and drone work; documents classifier deployment; warns open-weights gap is closing.
* **Constraint:** Institutional fact only. Link out, do not mirror. No method, no TTP, no scores beyond high-level, no code.

### [FABLE26] Fable 5 / Mythos 5 suspension (2026-06-12) - Tier 1
* **Citation:** US export-control directive 2026-06-12 17:21 ET ordering suspension of foreign-national access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5; Anthropic global shutdown same evening. * **Constraint:** Institutional fact only. No jailbreak detail beyond Anthropic's public characterization.
