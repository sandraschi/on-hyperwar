# Cursor Assessment - on-hyperwar

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Assessor:** Cursor (Grok 4.6)
**Form:** independent editorial argument. Does not mutate
`docs/ASSESSMENT.md` (2026-09-09) or the Gemini changelog of this date.
**Subject:** decision speed as the weapon; control over the kill chain,
ordered by reversibility.

Gemini's symposium note of today reports Rule 2 as "strictly enforced"
and lorecheck 44/44 as a fix. Both statements are true of the *gates*
and false of the *prose*. This assessment starts there.

---

## 1. One-sentence judgment

The thesis is load-bearing ("hyperwar is a clock speed, not a weapon
class") and Chapter I is the right question. The ladder that would make
the question checkable is internally inconsistent, empirically
under-sourced in Bands III-VI, and in places already a Rule 2 leak.
This is the most dangerous book in the tetralogy to get wrong, and
currently the most dangerous to publish as-is.

---

## 2. What the book actually is

Audience, as claimed: principals who can attach brakes to kill chains,
not hobbyists. Method ancestor: Kahn. Voice ancestor: Allen 2016-2024.
Empirical crucible: Ukraine 2022-2026, which inverted Allen's exquisite
arsenal into a Shenzhen dual-use catalog.

The three slides in Chapter I are the entire intellectual product,
stated once, then supposed to be dated per system:

- **In the loop:** human decides each release.
- **On the loop:** machine nominates, human approves - recruit in a
  dugout at 05:00, tenth red press of the night.
- **Told afterwards:** machine releases, human reads the log.

Doctrine claims the first. Practice lives in the second. The third
arrives without announcement.

Sister: on-ai-takeover (named, coupled, correct). on-hyperart and
on-metacraft are not in this book's vocabulary. They should be, at the
level of *clock*, not at the level of munitions.

Status drift: README still says construction v0.1 (2026-09-09);
CHANGELOG tops at v0.3 (2026-09-10); Gemini notes 2026-10-13. The
front door is a month behind the house.

## 5. Gaps that matter

**Gap 1 - Chapter V is not using Chapter IV's ladder.**
This is not a nit. It is a lore-check fail that `just lore` cannot
see because lorecheck counts rung *mentions*, not rung *identity*.
Coverage 44/44 with a mistranslated present is how this fleet
produces gates that examined nothing. Examples flagged this pass:
Ch V "Rung 10 = sensor-fused nomination" versus Ch IV "Rung 10 =
SGR-A1 sentry consent gate"; Ch V band labels (Assisted/Supervised)
are one band off Ch IV (Assisted is Band I). Until this is repaired,
"locating the present" is a mood.

**Gap 2 - Gemini's Rule 2 claim is false of the text.**
CONTRIBUTING forbids frequencies, TTPs, build guides, targeting
detail, ranges/payloads beyond manufacturer publication. The
anatomies in IV still read as mechanism: solenoid, recoil travel,
killflash, breaching, named silicon. II and VII still carry
frequency bands. ILLUSTRATIONS.md admits fabricated patent CAD
(RQU-7, UC-RQ4) while the site still embeds patent-style images.
A chapter that teaches gets cut. Several pages teach. Cursor
disagrees with Gemini's Security section of today's note. The
*intention* of Rule 2 is intact. The *prose* is not.

**Gap 3 - Bands III-VI are narrated as weather and flagged as
construction.**
That combination is how prophecy sneaks in through the servants'
entrance. Either date the rung or keep the flag in the sentence
when the rung is used outside IV. Present-location claims that
depend on unevidenced rungs 16-22 should not be written as
"squarely in."

**Gap 4 - Chapter III is a stub in the wrong book.**
Takeover already wrote "no equation of state" properly (Ord, Tn,
Castle Bravo as the checkable failure). Hyperwar's substitute
metric is sitting in VII waiting to be promoted: nomination-to-
consent histograms, rejection rates, operator workload. Until
those exist in public, HITL is PR. That *is* the equation of
state, and it is unwritten.

**Gap 5 - Day Two is empty.**
Kahn was paid to think about aftermath. A hyperwar book that
stops at terminal-state names without war-termination,
verification-at-violation-speed, and hotlines that work at
machine speed is a ladder with no way down. IX is fifty words.
That is not a chapter. It is an apology.

**Gap 6 - Threshold owners are unnamed.**
Ch VI says each threshold needs a named owner. None have one.
A threshold without an owner is a poster.

**Gap 7 - Series blindness.**
No hyperart, no metacraft. The 2026 story is that the same labor-
cost collapse cloning Photoshop can clone the *software layer of
command* - and that sentence is exactly as far as this book may
go. Not how. That it is now cheap. Metacraft's oracle rule is
the missing physics for "when does HITL become ceremonial": when
checking a nomination costs more milliseconds than the munition
has left.

## 7. Cursor opinions

### 7.1 Clock speed is the tetralogy's master key.

Hyperwar said it first and said it best. Takeover's faster-clock
coupling, hyperart's iron-triangle collapse, metacraft's
agent-hour budget are the same sentence in other uniforms. This
book should not expand to swallow them. It should *export* the
sentence in the preface: four clocks, one ordering principle
(reversibility of human control), four surfaces.

### 7.2 Fiber week and PhotoCraft week are the same shape.

Summer 2026, Donetsk: a DJI-class airframe, kilometers of fiber,
no radio, weekly tit-for-tat, no requirement document.
September 30 2026, GitHub: a Photoshop clean-room, daily
releases, agent co-authors, no Adobe permission.

Both are dual-use stacks beating exquisite incumbents. Both make
export control and IP control look like doctrines written for a
prior clock. The coupling is analogical and institutional, never
technical. Write one paragraph in X. Do not write a craft.

### 7.3 The moral crumple zone is already a product feature.

In software it is called "human in the loop" and sold as safety.
In war it is called "positive control" and sold as law. The
dugout recruit and the PhotoCraft user clicking through an
agent-generated action list are the same role: a named human
whose latency is longer than the process and whose function is
to absorb blame. Hyperwar discovered this. Metacraft should cite
it. Takeover already has "ceremonial oversight" (rung 35) and
should date the war case as an early exhibit, not a metaphor.

### 7.4 Do not let the scifi corollary become the book.

~4,000 words of Lem/Dick/Watts/Clarke/Vinge/Egan is allowed as
corollary. It is currently one of the longest files. Kahn-flat
fails most often where the prose enjoys itself. Keep the
corollary. Shorten it if IV anatomies are cut and III/IX need
the pages. The principals who can attach brakes will not be
persuaded by Watts. They will be persuaded by a histogram.

### 7.5 Threshold B is the one that matters, and it is not a
weapons threshold.

Ch IV's B: human approval latency shorter than human cognitive
reaction time, oversight as administrative fiction. That is an
*audit* threshold wearing a uniform. It is takeover's Audit
Floor (also B) in a dugout. Align the names across books or
stop pretending the letters are a shared language. Right now A-E
mean different things in hyperwar IV, hyperwar VI, and takeover
VI. Shared letters, unshared meanings, is how the series
confuses its own readers - including its own models.

### 7.6 The fabricated patent drawings are a credibility landmine.

A book that demands dated public facts cannot include fake
documentary authority "for atmosphere." ILLUSTRATIONS.md is
honest. The embedded images are not. Kill them or label them as
archetype diagrams with no patent number, no NSN, no implied
procurement. This is Rule 1, not aesthetics.

## 10. Verdict

**Do not publish. Do not treat lorecheck 44/44 as health. Repair
the ladder identity, cut the accelerator pages, write Day Two.**

The question in Chapter I is good enough to justify the repo.

colonel, and it should not. Reisner's unread memo is the correct
next reader. Gemini's changelog is too kind on security and too
impressed by coverage gates. Coverage is the fleet's most common
failure mode. This book is currently an exhibit of it.
