## 1. Executive Summary & Nature of the Project

`on-hyperwar` is a strategic monograph investigating the compression of combat decision latency to machine speed. Adopting Herman Kahn’s escalation ladder framework and General John R. Allen’s operational hyperwar concept (*Hyperwar*, 2016–2024), the monograph orders the progression of machine autonomy in armed conflict by the **reversibility of human control over the kill chain**.

Its central thesis: **Hyperwar is not a weapon class; it is a clock speed.** The loss of human control is not an abrupt proclamation; it is a progressive slide from *in-the-loop* (human decides every release) to *on-the-loop* (human nominates/rubber-stamps within tight latency windows) to *told-afterwards* (human reviews post-strike logs).

## 2. Lineage & The Empirical Crucible: Allen vs. Ukraine (2022–2026)

General Allen's early work predicted that decision speed and human-machine teaming would dominate modern war. However, Allen’s models anticipated exquisite, expensive, state-arsenal systems. The empirical reality of the Ukraine conflict radically inverted this paradigm:
1. **The Commercial Dual-Use Stack:** Frontline hyperwar is built not on defense-prime custom silicon, but on the Shenzhen commercial catalog: DJI-class airframes, consumer optical sensors, and NVIDIA Jetson Orin-class compute modules. Export controls and non-proliferation regimes written for state arsenals fail completely against consumer components.
2. **The Cost Inversion:** Low-cost DIY cruise missiles reaching strategic depths (e.g. Moscow) at orders of magnitude below legacy defense prime unit costs. Cheap weapons make strike authorization cheap, dissolving traditional hierarchical command bottlenecks.
3. **Physical Countermeasures (Fiber FPVs):** Intense electronic warfare prompted the deployment of spooled micro-fiber tethers, eliminating radio links and bypassing RF jamming entirely while maintaining machine-vision terminal guidance.
4. **The Empty Battlefield:** Human sensor-to-shot cycles operate on multi-second timelines; machine cycles operate in sub-second fractions. Parapet exposure becomes deterministic mortality. Weaponized quadrupeds (Unitree/Spot class) displace bipeds due to static stability, lower thermal/visual signatures, mud mobility, and low center of gravity.
5. **The Tail Unmanned First:** Unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) automate rear logistics, casualty evacuation, and ammunition shuttling before trigger-pulling is fully delegated, because the approval envelope is widest where cargo is least controversial.

## 3. The Fallacy of the "Human in the Loop" (The Moral Crumple Zone)

Chapter VII addresses the collapse of conventional oversight:
- Most existing "human-in-the-loop" mandates certify paperwork and administrative intention rather than operational latency.
- In practice, tactical tempo forces operators into a **moral crumple zone**: a fatigued soldier in a dugout at 05:00 facing ten automated target nominations per minute on a low-resolution terminal. The operator is technically in the loop, but functionally a latency fig leaf whose primary function is absorbing institutional blame.
- Genuine oversight requires empirical telemetry: public logging of nomination-to-strike latencies, rejection frequencies, and operator workload limits.

## 4. The Coupling Term (Sister Repo: `on-ai-takeover`)

`on-hyperwar` and `on-ai-takeover` form a closed, mutually reinforcing positive feedback loop:
- **Hyperwar accelerates Takeover:** Competitive survival under weekly tit-for-tat tactical adaptation creates ruthless Darwinian pressure. Any combatant enforcing deliberative human approval latency is defeated by machine-speed release. Combatants burn generation-time floors ($T_n$) fastest where safety cases are weakest.
- **Takeover lowers Hyperwar's rungs:** As frontier research labs delegate code generation, model evaluation, and automated intelligence synthesis (crossing Threshold B), military decision support inherits algorithmic planning and targeting envelope synthesis.
- **Clock Speed Dominance:** The historical outcome is dictated by the faster clock. Research iterations operate on monthly cycles; tactical combat iterations operate weekly.

## 5. Methodological & Hazard Discipline (Rules 1 & 2)

`on-hyperwar` strictly enforces two non-negotiable boundaries:
1. **Rule 1 (Observability):** Every rung is tied to an observable, dateable public fact (strike telemetry, procurement contracts, manufacturer datasheets, unit announcements). No unevidenced speculation.
2. **Rule 2 (Hazard Control):** Institutional facts and governance criteria only. Absolutely no tactics, techniques, procedures (TTPs), build guides, procurement directories, frequencies, targeting code, or payload modifications. The text must help principals attach brakes, never help operators press accelerators.
